# Agent instructions for this repository

This repository is a reusable skill called **Field Read**: a method for reading a whole field (an industry, a technology wave, a profession) from the public record alone, over a 2 to 5 year horizon, and for comparing two or three fields side by side when asked.

When a task asks where a field is going, whether one field is a better place to be than another, what happens to a kind of role when a technology lands, or for an outside view before a career, product or investment decision that depends on how a field moves, read `skills/field-read/SKILL.md` first and follow it. The lane briefs are in `skills/field-read/references/lanes.md`, the sources in `skills/field-read/references/sources.md`, and the output layouts (one field, or a comparison) in `skills/field-read/references/outputs.md`. Sample reads are in `skills/field-read/examples/`. For a single company, use Cold Read instead.

Non-negotiable rules from the skill: public record only; mark every claim ✅ cited, 🟡 inferred or ❔ unknown; never open a person's LinkedIn profile; hype is a data point, not a finding; base rates before forecasts; every scenario carries a dated confirming signal and a killing signal; comparisons are symmetric in horizon, lanes and depth; keep any inside knowledge in a separate, clearly marked section; date everything.
