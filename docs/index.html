A field cannot hide its direction

It is in where the money goes, what the job boards ask for, how fast the capability line moves and what it costs, what the largest players say against what they ship, what the regulators and standards bodies date, what happened the last three times, and where credible people still disagree. Field Read reads those seven places and writes one note: the answer first, then the evidence, the base rate, three dated scenarios, the roles that gain and lose, what it means for you, and the dates that would prove it wrong.

  • CapitalWhere the money is going, at what stage and valuation; exits and failures. Money moves 18 to 36 months before headcount.Funding databases, fund announcements, comparables and multiples, shutdown trackers, grants.
  • LabourWhich roles the field is hiring and has stopped hiring, where, at what seniority and pay. Job boards across the field are next year's org charts.Applicant-tracking APIs across 15 to 30 named companies, layoff trackers, labour statistics, pay bands.
  • Capability curveWhere the technology line is, how fast it moves, and what one unit of work costs now against two years ago.Releases, third-party benchmarks, pricing pages and their archives, open-source activity, field tests.
  • IncumbentsWhat the five largest players tell investors against what they ship, and which adjacent markets they enter. The gap is the platform risk for everyone smaller.Earnings calls, shareholder letters, release notes, developer conferences, pricing changes.
  • Regulation and standardsThe laws, safety standards, procurement rules and liability positions that set the pace more often than the technology does.Legislation and consultations, standards bodies, enforcement pages, court records, insurer positions.
  • Base ratesWhat happened to the same roles and company types in the last three comparable shifts. Written before any scenario, and every scenario must respect it or say why this time differs.Data-rich histories, labour series, funding and failure data, archived job boards, contemporaneous analyst notes.
  • DisagreementWhere credible, named people disagree, and what evidence would settle each dispute. Uncertainty is recorded, not averaged away.Essays, papers, talks, dated forecasts. No LinkedIn profile is ever opened.

Four questions it is built for

Career

Is this a good field to move into, and which roles gain?

Ten roles that gain and ten that lose, the hiring vocabulary, the gap between you and the gaining roles, three questions to answer before moving. Never "go" or "stay".

Build

Where will the capability line be when my product ships, and who is moving in?

The capability curve, the incumbents' says and does, kill criteria as dated signals.

Invest

Where is the money going, and what does it expect back?

Capital by quarter, comparables and multiples, the bull and the bear by name.

Position

Where should a company stand in a moving field?

The market map by scenario, the two or three dated events that change the ranking.

Comparisons, when you want one

A single field is the default. Ask for "A versus B" and the skill reads both fields with the same lanes, the same horizon and the same depth, and writes a second layout: your criteria first (written down before the lanes run, so they cannot drift to fit the answer), the fields side by side, scenarios per field, a criteria table with a mark in every cell, the cost of switching in each direction, the option value each field keeps, and the dated decision points. The honest result is often "A on criteria one to four, B on five to seven, and it turns on criterion three", with the signal that settles it.

  • Same horizon on every field, or the note says which side is thinner and why.
  • The reader's weights are the reader's; the note never invents them.
  • Switching costs are written in both directions, because the field you already know always looks riskier.

Install it in your agent

Pick your agent. Two minutes, no account.

One line, as a plugin. The skill then answers to /field-read.

/plugin install field-read --marketplace me2resh/field-read

Older versions: /plugin marketplace add me2resh/field-read, then /plugin install field-read@field-read. From a shell: claude plugin install field-read --marketplace me2resh/field-read. Update later with /plugin update field-read@field-read.

Codex reads skills from ~/.codex/skills. The installer copies the folder there.

git clone https://github.com/me2resh/field-read
cd field-read
./install.sh codex

Inside a project, AGENTS.md in the repository is also written for Codex: copy its text into your project's AGENTS.md and keep skills/field-read/ where Codex can read it.

Cursor uses project rules. Copy the rule file into your project; it loads the skill when a task matches.

git clone https://github.com/me2resh/field-read
mkdir -p .cursor/rules
cp field-read/.cursor/rules/field-read.mdc .cursor/rules/
cp -R field-read/skills/field-read .cursor/

The rule points at skills/field-read/SKILL.md; keep the copied folder next to it or adjust the path in the rule.

Copilot reads repository instructions. Copy the instructions file and the skill folder into your project.

git clone https://github.com/me2resh/field-read
mkdir -p .github
cp field-read/.github/copilot-instructions.md .github/
cp -R field-read/skills/field-read .github/

If your project already has copilot-instructions.md, append the text instead of replacing it.

Any agent that reads a folder with a SKILL.md, or a plain instructions file, can use it.

git clone https://github.com/me2resh/field-read
cd field-read
./install.sh /path/to/your/agent/skills

For agents with a single instructions file, paste AGENTS.md into it and keep skills/field-read/ readable by the agent.

Then ask the way you would ask a colleague.

  • Field read industrial robotics software over three years. Career lens.
  • Compare platform engineering with physical AI as a place to spend the next three years.
  • What happens to engineering managers in 50 to 500 person software companies as coding agents mature?
  • Where is the money going in developer tools, and what does it expect back? Outside view only.

Seven things it will not do

  • Go behind a login.Filings, registries, press, job ads, public pages and APIs, benchmarks, archives. No scraping against terms, no pretexts.
  • State a claim without a mark.Cited and linked, inferred from the record, or unknown after looking. Readers tell research from hype at a glance.
  • Repeat hype as a finding.Funding totals, keynote claims and market-size figures go in with their source and their sponsor, never into the answer as facts about the field.
  • Forecast before the base rate.No scenario is written until the last three comparable shifts are on the page. A forecast that disagrees with them has to say why.
  • Write a scenario without a falsifier.Each one names the signals that confirm it, the date they should be visible, and the one signal that kills it.
  • Compare two fields unevenly.Same horizon, same lanes, same depth, criteria written before the lanes run. If one side got less time, the note says so.
  • Open a LinkedIn profile.A profile visit tells the person they are being researched. Public writing, talks and snippets instead.
